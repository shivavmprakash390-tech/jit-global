Frequently Asked

Healthcare technology questions, answered.

Understand how JIT Global approaches healthcare infrastructure, cybersecurity, modernization, compliance, and resilience.

How can JIT Global help healthcare organizations modernize their infrastructure? +−

JIT Global can help assess existing infrastructure, identify modernization opportunities, and design secure cloud, hybrid, or modern infrastructure environments aligned with business and operational requirements.

How do you protect sensitive healthcare data? +−

Security can be built across identity, access management, encryption, monitoring, infrastructure, applications, and data environments to reduce exposure and strengthen protection.

Can you support HIPAA-related security requirements? +−

JIT Global can help organizations implement security controls and technology practices aligned with healthcare privacy and security requirements. Specific compliance obligations should be assessed based on the organization’s environment and regulatory scope.

Can legacy healthcare applications be modernized? +−

Yes. Legacy workloads can be assessed and approached through rehosting, refactoring, modernization, integration, or phased replacement depending on business requirements.

How do you minimize disruption during healthcare technology transformation? +−

Transformation should be planned around business continuity, workload dependencies, risk, testing, phased migration, rollback planning, and operational requirements.

Can JIT Global provide ongoing infrastructure and security support? +−

Yes. Managed infrastructure, monitoring, optimization, security, backup, resilience, and ongoing technical support can be incorporated into the engagement.
