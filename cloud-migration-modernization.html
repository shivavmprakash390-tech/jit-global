FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does JIT Global approach cloud migration? +−
We begin by understanding your existing infrastructure, applications, dependencies, security requirements, and business objectives. We then create a practical migration roadmap covering assessment, planning, migration, validation, and post-migration optimization.
What types of workloads can you migrate to the cloud? +−
We support a wide range of workloads, including servers, applications, databases, enterprise systems, storage, and data environments. The migration approach is selected based on workload complexity, business criticality, dependencies, and modernization goals.
Can you modernize applications instead of simply migrating them? +−
Yes. Not every workload should be moved to the cloud exactly as it exists today. Where appropriate, we help organizations modernize applications and architectures to improve scalability, performance, resilience, maintainability, and cloud efficiency.
Which cloud platforms do you support? +−
Our cloud capabilities span major platforms including AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud, allowing us to align the technology choice with your business and technical requirements.
How do you minimize disruption during migration? +−
We use structured discovery, dependency mapping, migration planning, testing, validation, phased execution, and rollback planning to reduce operational risk and minimize business disruption.
Can JIT Global manage our cloud environment after migration? +−
Yes. Cloud transformation can extend beyond migration into ongoing infrastructure management, monitoring, optimization, security, backup, performance management, and operational support.
